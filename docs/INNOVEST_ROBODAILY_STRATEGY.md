# Innovest / RoboDaily — Brand & operating model

**Decision recorded:** 2026-11-07. Founder intends **Innovest** as the umbrella business and **RoboDaily** as the customer-facing brand. RoboLap (GitHub repository spelling: RoboLap) is the open technical R&D platform. These are strategic working names, not evidence of incorporated entities or registered trademarks.

## Proposed structure

- **Innovest** — future legal entity / group umbrella. Owns or properly licenses commercial IP, manages funding, investment, financial control and partner agreements.
- **RoboLap** — technical R&D and open-source robotics framework: hardware abstraction, vision, skills, safety engineering, datasets and experimental prototypes.
- **RoboDaily** — commercial go-to-market brand for Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS), robot fleet operations, customer service and eventual franchise partners.

**Suggested customer promise:** "Robots at work. Every day." Slogan is provisional and requires trademark/competitive checks.

## Legal and IP cautions

1. **Not incorporated / registered by this document.** Confirm Innovest legal entity status, registered seat, company purpose, trade names and tax position.
2. **Trademark clearance:** Search DPMAregister, EUIPO/TMview and relevant international registries for Innovest and RoboDaily, including similar marks and relevant Nice classes (potentially 7, 9, 37, 42 and 35; specialist to determine exact coverage). Check domain names and competitive use. Do not claim exclusive rights until verified.
3. **Open source vs commercial IP:** GitHub public visibility does not grant an open-source license. Choose a suitable license for RoboLap and distinguish third-party contributions, brand/trademarks, customer data, proprietary deployment configurations and commercial service contracts. Consider contributor agreements if needed; do not assume all public code is exclusive Innovest IP.
4. **Employment and conflict clearance:** Before formal commercial operation, obtain applicable employer side-activity / IP clearance and check contracts, resources and confidentiality. Do not use employer assets or proprietary information.
5. **Franchise:** Prove unit economics in a directly operated pilot before promising franchise earnings. Document operator safety, machine conformity, servicing, insurance and financing responsibilities.
6. **Funding:** Separate Innovest's own R&D costs from RoboDaily's commercial fleet investments. Check affiliated-enterprise, company-size and state-aid rules, and no double-funding of identical costs. Never assume a brand name alone can receive grants.

## Business model

**Customer:** pays monthly for a defined service outcome, robot availability and support.

**Regional partner:** handles installation, customer support and operation, potentially finances robots via approved leasing partners.

**RoboDaily HQ:** receives platform/service fees and eventually franchise royalties; provides training, approved vendor catalog, service standards, reporting and commercial agreements.

**Innovest:** allocates capital, manages the IP and investment/funding strategy; keeps fleet risk asset-light where commercially feasible.

## Roadmap and gates

- **Gate 0 — Identity:** verify naming, IP ownership, company structure, employer constraints and open-source license.
- **Gate 1 — RoboLap:** safe lab demo with two SO-101 follower arms; validate reusable skills across two object classes.
- **Gate 2 — RoboDaily pilot:** pick a certified commercially suitable robot category (cleaning or industrial sorting), secure three customer expressions of interest, validate service and leasing economics.
- **Gate 3 — Finance:** pursue eligible R&D grants/tax credit at Innovest and separate bank/leasing finance for commercial fleets; do not place binding orders before funding/project-start checks.
- **Gate 4 — Replication:** operate and document six months of unit economics; test one external regional service partner.
- **Gate 5 — Franchise:** roll out only after reproducible profitability, operating manual, contractual and regulatory review.
