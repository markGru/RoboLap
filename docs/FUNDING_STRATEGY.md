# RoboLap financing & public funding strategy — DE / Niedersachsen

**Research date:** 2026-11-07. **Status:** planning concept, not an approval or application. Re-check program status and legal requirements before spending or applying.

## Operating thesis

RoboLap should become a vendor-agnostic robotics software and service platform. Keep **three distinct budget pools**:

1. **R&D / IP** — safety-aware device adapters, computer vision, bimanual control, reusable skills, teleoperation and reproducible evaluations. Finance from owner funds, eligible innovation grants and research tax credit (subject to eligibility).
2. **First commercial robot fleet** — certified commercially suitable robots purchased with asset financing, supplier financing or leasing. Funding loans may support eligible acquisition/operating needs. Confirm whether machines primarily **leased onward to customers** are eligible for each financing product.
3. **Franchise roll-out** — regional partners finance their own fleet and working capital, while RoboLap central earns licensing and platform/service fees. Prefer an asset-light HQ and centrally negotiated financing arrangements.

The two SO-101 lab arms are experimental prototypes: they are NOT immediately commercially deployable or compliant rental work equipment.

## Illustrative, not approved: EUR 150k R&D program

- Project: novel transferable skill library and safe robot abstraction + 2-arm grasping evaluation.
- Example eligible budget: **EUR 150k** (personnel EUR 100k, other eligible EUR 50k; actual eligible categories and rates must be checked).
- **Hypothetical** NBank 45% on fully eligible costs: **EUR 67.5k** grant.
- Self-financing / bridge financing: **EUR 82.5k**. Plus liquidity timing buffer.
- This is conditional; do **not** claim EUR 67.5k until the funding body grants it.
- For financed costs, track documented invoices, personnel timesheets and public contributions separately. Evaluate FZul only for costs not double-funded.

## Illustrative robot-rental pilot: EUR 100k capital needs

- Three professionally suitable work robots at a planning cost of EUR 20k each: **EUR 60k**.
- Commissioning, training and compliance preparation: **EUR 15k**.
- Sales/customer onboarding: **EUR 10k**.
- Operating cash reserve: **EUR 15k**.
- **EUR 100k total capital requirement** before any contingency beyond that reserve.
- *Possible financing mix, to be negotiated and not confirmed*: EUR 15k owners' cash, EUR 60k asset lease/finance, EUR 25k founder credit. Keep R&D grants OUT of fleet financing unless the approved grant explicitly permits a relevant expenditure.
- Analyze unit economics per robot: financed asset charge, monthly service, repairs, insurance, transport, occupancy, customer churn, safety compliance and cash collection. Model a downside scenario with only 60% of planned utilization.

## Franchise financing product

- Regional partners choose finance or own fleets, subject to bank/lessor approval. RoboLap HQ standardizes approved models, onboarding, software subscriptions and field support.
- Request leasing / equipment finance quotes for 1, 3, 5, 10 robots; compare operating lease vs purchase with secured loan vs vendor finance.
- Avoid placing all physical robot residual-value and customer-default risk at HQ.
- Include insurance, service guarantees, robotics safety and commercial use rules in franchise standards.
- Validate finance products' eligibility for robots rented onward; this cannot be assumed from general machine-purchase eligibility.

## Gates — NO premature commitments

**Gate 0: funding & IP clearance**
- Resolve any employee side-activity, ownership/IP and conflicts-of-interest questions before submitting for external funding.
- Obtain IHK Braunschweig and NBank advisory calls; verify program openness, R&D novelty and regional rules.
- Separate cost centers and decide whether future investor-readiness requires a GmbH.
- Decide public code license explicitly; do not assume public GitHub code has an open-source license.

**Gate 1: tech MVP**
- Two simulation-capable SO-101 followers, real grasp test and repeatability logs.
- Write an eligibility-ready R&D project description with technical uncertainties and milestones.

**Gate 2: commercial pilot**
- Obtain two manufacturer/service offers for commercially deployable robots and at least three customer letters of intent.
- Confirm financing for assets *before* signing binding purchase orders.
- Establish compliant safe deployment and operator training; price commercial service from verified unit economics.

**Gate 3: franchise replication**
- At least six months of measured pilot economics and reliable maintenance documentation.
- Replicate training, customer sales, financing, service and escalation with an independent partner.
- Only then charge franchise fees / expand.

**Critical:** Each program has its own legal definition of project start. NBank 2026 rules sometimes permit at-risk start after filing for certain projects under EUR 100k, but NEVER assume general permission. Get written confirmation of the applicable rule before placing binding purchase/implementation orders.
